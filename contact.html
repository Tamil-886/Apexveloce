FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Consultation & Studio Discovery

Everything you need to know about booking, paint inspection, and vehicle handover.

How does the bespoke design consultation work?

You can start by submitting our online 4-step wizard or calling our direct hotline. A dedicated master stylist will review your vehicle specs, discuss vinyl swatch samples, prepare digital CAD mockups, and provide a binding fixed-cost proposal before scheduling cleanroom bay time.

Can I inspect physical vinyl swatches & PPF finishes in person?

Yes! We invite clients to our Beverly Hills Studio design lounge where you can hold physical swatches from 3M, Avery Dennison, Inozetek, KPMF, and XPEL against your car under high-CRI Lutron studio lights to evaluate true depth, metallic flake, and gloss levels.

What is the typical studio turnaround time for a full wrap or PPF?

A full color change wrap or track-pack PPF installation typically requires 4 to 6 studio business days. This includes multi-stage chemical decontamination, edge disassemblies, precision knifeless application, edge post-heating at 90°C, and 24-hour climate curing.

Do you offer white-glove transport and airport concierge?

Yes. We operate a dedicated fleet of hydraulic-lift enclosed trailers serving Southern California and nationwide collection across the continental US. For fly-in clients, our chauffeur provides private transfers to/from Van Nuys (VNY) and LAX.

Is my vehicle fully insured while at the studio?

Apex Veloce carries comprehensive garage-keeper and exotic vehicle coverage up to $10,000,000 per bay. Our facility is secured by 24/7 biometric access controls, armed response telemetry, and high-definition cleanroom surveillance.

Do you sign NDAs for confidential prototype or celebrity builds?

Yes. We regularly execute legal Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) for pre-production automotive manufacturer prototypes, SEMA show concepts, and high-profile private collectors. Photography in our cleanroom is strictly controlled upon request.
