WHITE-GLOVE EXECUTION

The 4-Stage Studio Process

From intake to final white-glove inspection, here is how we execute every bespoke build with motorsport rigor.

01

Intake & 3D Laser Scan

Complete 50-point baseline inspection, paint depth micrometer scanning, and digital template contour alignment.

02

Chemical Decontamination

Iron particle dissolution, fine clay bar scrub, IPA sterilization, and single-stage paint jeweling to guarantee 100% surface bond.

03

Cleanroom Bay Installation

Tension-free application inside ISO-7 filtered bays followed by 90°C shortwave infrared post-heat stabilization.

04

100-Point Light Tunnel QC

Final inspection under 5,000K high-CRI light bars, ceramic sealant infusion, and issuance of certified studio warranty card.
