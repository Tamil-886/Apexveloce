OUR ORIGIN & VISION

WHERE SURGICAL DISCIPLINE MEETS COACHWORK ARTISTRY

Apex Veloce was established in Beverly Hills by master coachbuilder Julian Vance and motorsport aerodynamicist Liam Cross. Frustrated by generic restyling shops using razor blades directly on OEM paint and cutting corners on edge tucking, they set out to establish a state-of-the-art laboratory dedicated to zero-compromise vehicle transformations.

Today, our 15,000 sq.ft facility features positive-pressure HEPA cleanrooms, an engineered 100-point LED light tunnel, and computerized plotting suites to guarantee every exotic vehicle leaves in showroom condition.

BENCHMARK STANDARDS

OUR QUALITY ASSURANCE CHARTER

We uphold strict manufacturing tolerances to ensure complete satisfaction on every build.

Post-Heat Thermo Setting

Every edge is thermo-cured to 90°C with digital infrared sensors, locking the vinyl memory to eliminate lifting or peeling.

100-Point Light Tunnel

Vehicles pass through our CRI 98+ high-intensity inspection bay to expose any micro-bubbles, dust, or edge imperfections.

Computerized CAD Plotting

Pre-cut precision film templates designed specifically for each vehicle VIN to eliminate blades touching paintwork.

Transferable Warranty

All wraps and PPF packages come registered with direct manufacturer warranties, transferable if you sell your vehicle.
