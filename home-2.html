STUDIO EXCELLENCE

WHY CHOOSE OUR STUDIO

Expert Installation

Premium Materials

STEP-BY-STEP

OUR 6-STAGE BESPOKE PROCESS

From initial design consultation to cleanroom execution and final VIP handover.

01

Consult

VIP discovery session to discuss your aesthetic vision, inspect vehicle paint condition, and select material swatches.

02

Design

3D photorealistic CAD rendering, aerodynamic aero mapping, and custom livery vector artwork approval.

03

Prepare

Multi-stage decontamination wash, clay bar, panel trim removal, and cleanroom isopropanol surface prep.

04

Install

Tension-free cast vinyl application, deep corner edge-tucking, and 90°C post-heat thermo curing.

05

Inspect

100-point inspection under high-CRI light tunnel for optical clarity, seam integrity, and zero trapped particles.

06

Deliver

Final ceramic sealant topcoat application, warranty certificate handover, and executive vehicle unveiling.
