FREQUENTLY ASKED

PRICING & WARRANTY FAQ

Clear answers regarding deposits, material warranties, loaner vehicles, and paint inspections.

Our pricing is determined by three core variables: vehicle surface area (coupe vs. large SUV), body geometry complexity (standard vs. aero-heavy exotic), and selected material tier (cast vinyl, color shift, or self-healing XPEL PPF). Every quote is 100% written and guaranteed with zero surprise surcharges.

Our comprehensive warranty covers film lifting, edge peeling, bubbling, and UV colorfastness. Additionally, XPEL paint protection films carry a 10-year manufacturer warranty against yellowing, cracking, and hazing. All warranty certificates are registered directly in our digital studio vault.

Yes! We frequently execute hybrid transformations where we apply a full cast color change wrap, followed by XPEL Stealth or Ultimate Plus PPF on high-impact strike zones (front bumper, hood, rocker panels) for unbeatable aesthetics and rock-chip armor.

Every full wrap, PPF, and ceramic package includes our multi-stage cleanroom prep protocol: chemical iron fallout removal, fine clay bar decontamination, and isopropanol surface sterilization. Paint correction stages are bundled as specified in each tier.

A 20% deposit locks in your dedicated cleanroom bay and initiates material batch allocation from 3M, Inozetek, or XPEL. The remaining balance is due upon final vehicle inspection and VIP unveiling.
