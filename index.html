CLIENT KNOWLEDGE BASE

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Essential guidance on vehicle wrap longevity, PPF self-healing capabilities, warranties, and cleanroom care protocols.

Will wrapping or PPF damage my vehicle's original factory paint?

No. When applied over sound OEM factory paint, premium cast vinyl and XPEL paint protection films act as a protective barrier against UV oxidation, micro-abrasions, and road debris. Furthermore, our studio utilizes 100% digital CAD laser plotting and knifeless tape, ensuring zero razor blades ever touch your vehicle's clear coat. When removed by our technicians, your original factory paint is revealed in pristine condition.

What is the difference between a Vinyl Wrap and Paint Protection Film (PPF)?

Vinyl Wraps (90–110 microns) are primarily aesthetic films engineered for dramatic color changes, satin sheens, textures, or custom liveries.

Paint Protection Film (PPF, 200 microns / 8.5–10 mil) is a thick, elastomeric polyurethane armor engineered specifically for impact absorption against high-velocity rock chips, road gravel, and scratching, featuring true self-healing topcoats. Many clients choose a full color wrap topped with Track Pack PPF on high-impact zones.

How does the self-healing technology on XPEL PPF work?

XPEL Ultimate Plus utilizes advanced elastomeric polymers with memory retention in its clear coat. When fine scratches, wash swirls, or surface abrasions occur, exposure to direct sunlight, warm engine heat, or hot water causes the polymers to relax and reflow into their original optical state within seconds—completely erasing the blemishes.

How long does a full vehicle wrap or PPF installation take?

A bespoke full color change wrap or full body PPF installation typically requires 3 to 5 business days. This timeline includes our rigorous 24-hour decontamination & paint correction stage, precision cleanroom application with deep edge tucking, and a full 24-hour 90°C post-heat curing and 100-point inspection cycle before vehicle handover.

How should I wash and maintain my wrapped vehicle?

We recommend touchless washes or two-bucket hand washing using pH-neutral automotive shampoos and microfiber wash mitts. Avoid harsh abrasive polishes or automated brush car washes. For maximum longevity and effortless cleaning, we highly recommend our 9H Ceramic Coating topcoat package, which provides permanent hydrophobicity and dirt repellency over vinyl and PPF.
